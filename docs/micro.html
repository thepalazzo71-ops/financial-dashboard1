Scoring pool Every score on this page is computed against the entire pool for the active sector view (184 all-sector or 175 non-financial survivors) — the same percentile-ranking basis the methodology uses — regardless of the display filters above (search, country, top-N) which only control what's shown, not what's ranked against what. Switching the Sector selector swaps the whole pool, not just the display. What's fixed vs. live Revenue consistency, track record, dilution, profitability, cash-flow, revenue growth and dividend consistency are computed directly from the Capital IQ financial history and don't change here. Gross margin (18 pts, banded) and valuation (18 pts, best-of-three lenses: GM-adjusted P/S, ROE-adjusted P/B, growth-adjusted earnings yield) recompute live from whatever market cap and gross margin you enter. Companies without a verified gross margin use the pool's median GM-band score as a neutral fallback, and are flagged needs GM so you can prioritise them. This page is static — how updates actually happen This is a plain, self-contained HTML file with no server and no database: it was generated from the data in this repository's data-micro/ folder at build time. Typing a new market cap or gross margin into the table only changes what you see, in this browser — it's saved to this device's local storage (not shared, not synced anywhere) so it survives a reload, but nobody else who opens this page will see it, and it disappears if you clear site data. Use it freely to try scenarios.

This market group is brand new: market caps, theses, corporate actions and gross margins have NOT been researched yet - everything is still the original screening extract (market caps are as of the extract, and micro-cap prices move a lot), unlike the Europe and USA/Canada dashboards which have been through many live refresh batches. The Update market caps / Update gross margins buttons build you a ready-to-paste list of current values in the format a future data-micro/mc_overrides_applied.json would need, once that workflow exists. Formula note Dilution banding, the growth power-curve, and percentile tie-handling are this dashboard's best-effort reimplementation of the methodology document. Gross margin coverage for this pool (none verified yet) is much thinner than Europe's — expect scores and ranks for companies outside the known top-150 shortlists to differ more than "a place or two" from any prior run, since more of the pool relies on the median-GM fallback than the original methodology used.

Delisted / taken private

Companies whose stock is no longer tradable because a takeover, squeeze-out or delisting has completed. They are hidden from the main table and the movers list, but still sit in the scoring pool of the active sector view, so no other company's score or rank is affected (the rank shown here is where each would have ranked). Offers that are only announced or pending are NOT hidden — see Corporate actions.

Corporate actions

Tender offers, mergers/acquisitions, spinoffs and other restructuring found for companies in the shortlist range. A company under a pending corporate action is trading on deal terms, not fundamentals — its v6 score may no longer reflect a live value opportunity regardless of what it shows. Manually/agent-researched, not automated; a company not listed here simply hasn't turned up such an event, not necessarily that there is none.

Profitability bonus benefactors

This bonus is live in the real score. Rule: a company already in the top gross-margin band (≥60%, which already earns full gross-margin points) with long-term revenue growth (full-history CAGR >10%/year) that stays broadly cash-generative (CFO positive ≥70% of reported periods) gets its profitability score topped up to the full 16/16, regardless of how inconsistent its net income actually was — recognizing genuine high-margin growth stories that reinvest heavily rather than penalizing them for uneven GAAP profitability.

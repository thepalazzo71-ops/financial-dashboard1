Cheapest by P/E

Companies ranked by price / earnings = live market cap ÷ last-twelve-months net income (companies with positive earnings only; edit a market cap in the table and it updates here). EV / net profit is shown where net-debt data exists (EV = market cap + net debt). A very low P/E often means last-twelve-month profit contains a one-off gain (asset or investment sale): the P/E avg NI column uses the average of the last ~3 years of trailing earnings and NI spike flags companies whose latest profit is far above that average. Companies tagged est. cap have an estimated market cap. Follows the sector view (all sectors / non-financial) selected above; banks, insurers and other financial companies naturally show low P/Es, so switch to the non-financial view where there is one. This is a screen, not a score: it does not affect the v6 ranking. Click a company for its full detail.

Big swingers — widest 5-year price swings

Companies whose share price has swung the most over the last five years, measured on daily closing prices (split-adjusted, in the local trading currency, so the percentages are currency-neutral). Range = 5-year closing high ÷ 5-year closing low. “Deepest below peak” ranks by how far today’s price sits under the 5-year high; “Biggest rebound” by how far above the 5-year low.

Scoring pool Every score on this page is computed against the entire pool for the active sector view (517 all-sector or 361 non-financial survivors) — the same percentile-ranking basis the methodology uses — regardless of the display filters above (search, country, top-N) which only control what's shown, not what's ranked against what. Switching the Sector selector swaps the whole pool, not just the display. What's fixed vs. live Revenue consistency, track record, dilution, profitability, cash-flow, revenue growth and dividend consistency are computed directly from the Capital IQ financial history and don't change here. Gross margin (18 pts, banded) and valuation (18 pts, best-of-three lenses: GM-adjusted P/S, ROE-adjusted P/B, growth-adjusted earnings yield) recompute live from whatever market cap and gross margin you enter. Companies without a verified gross margin use the pool's median GM-band score as a neutral fallback, and are flagged needs GM so you can prioritise them. This page is static — how updates actually happen This is a plain, self-contained HTML file with no server and no database: it was generated from the data in this repository's data-us/ folder at build time. Typing a new market cap or gross margin into the table only changes what you see, in this browser — it's saved to this device's local storage (not shared, not synced anywhere) so it survives a reload, but nobody else who opens this page will see it, and it disappears if you clear site data. Use it freely to try scenarios.

No market-cap refresh pipeline has run yet for this market — everything shown is the original screening extract, unlike the Europe dashboard which has been through several live refresh batches. The Update market caps / Update gross margins buttons build you a ready-to-paste list of current values in the format a future data-us/mc_overrides_applied.json would need, once that workflow exists. Formula note Dilution banding, the growth power-curve, and percentile tie-handling are this dashboard's best-effort reimplementation of the methodology document. Gross margin coverage for this pool (~59%, vs. the original research's ~100%) is meaningfully thinner than Europe's — expect scores and ranks for companies outside the known top-150 shortlists to differ more than "a place or two" from any prior run, since more of the pool relies on the median-GM fallback than the original methodology used.

Update market caps

Pre-filled with the current 150 shortlist and their present market caps, in TICKER, value in $M format, so you don't have to assemble the list yourself. Research fresh values from a market-data source, paste them back over this box in the same format, and apply — this previews the effect in your browser only. To make it permanent, copy the same lines into data/mc_overrides_applied.json and rebuild (see the note below the table).

Update gross margins

Paste one company per line as TICKER, GM%, [fiscal year], [source note] — fiscal year and source are optional. Research gross margin from official filings only (annual reports, earnings releases, exchange filings — no third-party aggregators) for the tickers below. This previews the effect in your browser only; to make it permanent, copy the same lines into data/gm_overrides_applied.json and rebuild.

Corporate actions

Tender offers, mergers/acquisitions, spinoffs and other restructuring found for companies in the shortlist range. A company under a pending corporate action is trading on deal terms, not fundamentals — its v6 score may no longer reflect a live value opportunity regardless of what it shows. Manually/agent-researched, not automated; a company not listed here simply hasn't turned up such an event, not necessarily that there is none.
